# DataCo Business Insights

These findings are calculated from the uploaded Kaggle DataCo dataset.

## Dataset
- Rows: 180,519
- Distinct orders: 65,752
- Distinct customers: 20,652
- Direct customer/security fields were excluded from the analytics dataset.

## Executive KPIs
- Revenue: 36,784,735.01
- Profit: 3,966,902.97
- Profit margin: 10.78%
- On-time delivery: 17.84%
- Late delivery: 54.83%

## Initial findings
1. **Top revenue category:** Fishing with revenue of 6,929,653.69.
2. **Highest late-rate shipping mode:** First Class at 95.32%.
3. **Largest market by revenue:** Europe with revenue of 10,872,396.80.

## Recommendations
- Investigate the operational causes behind high late-delivery rates by shipping mode and market.
- Prioritize high-revenue categories while monitoring their profit margins.
- Use the monthly trend and MoM analysis to support demand and logistics planning.
- Segment customers and markets by both revenue and delivery performance rather than revenue alone.

## Important
These are descriptive findings, not causal conclusions. Additional operational data would be needed to establish why delays occur.
